# Practice

The Practice tab, its scheduler, drills and speaking exercises.

- `src/practice/`: the widget's Practice tab (lesson mode: between Lesson and Passage, on the lesson; page mode: after Passage, on the section on screen with the page's words and a grammar point for the reader's level, facts true to the page, and an exercise written ahead for another section asked for again): one exercise at a time, with Check (or Skip) and "Next exercise"; the next is written while the reader does this one, so it shows at once; "More like this" writes one of the same type on the same point (lesson) or section (page) and setting, the exercise on screen in the prompt's `<like>` so its sentences aren't reused (`planLike` in `schedule`), and the one written ahead waits for after it. Modelled on the ESL app mock-ups (one lesson per level, A1–C1). `exercises` (39 types, each with the CEFR levels it suits, its stage (words, forms, texts, talk), the languages it's for if not all (`langs`: `particles`, Japanese only, gaps for は, が, を, に, で…), what the prompt asks for, its JSON shape, `off` if it's out of the rotation (never planned, kept for evals: `spot_form`, whose keys missed uses of the form and which on a sound point was a hunt for letters), and a parser that checks it and shuffles its options; they draw as 9 kinds: choice, label, order, pairs, tap, proofread, fix, dialogue, speak (the opt-in `say` type, below); left out: timed rounds, and types built around one topic), `schedule` (`planNext`, run by the controller: what each exercise practises, looking random but working through everything: a kit point in clusters of 2–3 (1–2 once every point has had its turn), least practised first; a point answered wrong back after its cluster, as another type, twice running at most, this session only; 4 theme words, least practised first; a type from a working set that starts at 5 and grows every 3 exercises, its stage from a shuffled bag of the level's weights; page mode plans types only. What a lesson has practised is kept across sessions in `chrome.storage.local` (`coverage.ts`, the kit's points and words from a `practice-lesson` message) and cleared by the tab's "Start afresh"; what was answered, and how, is kept apart as history and not cleared (`ledger.ts`, one per language, `practice-ledger|<lang>`: per local day exercises answered and right, per lesson each point, word and type right and wrong with the first and last day right); `npm run eval -- schedule` simulates it against the old picker), `plan` (`pickExercise`, the old picker, kept as that baseline; the stage weights by level), `prompt` (one Sonnet call per exercise, thinking off, one line of JSON; `choosePractice` sets a lesson's in one of the kit's situations, on the planned point and words in `<focus>`, `choosePagePractice` takes the page's section; `parsePractice`; one that fails its checks is written once more, and so is one that gives its answer away, `giveaway.ts`, the same test for every language and type: a sort whose items show their own group (a pronoun, an article, an ending named as the group, a word of a pattern like "hace…" that only its group's items have), pairs whose halves show each other, a choice whose question or sentence has the answer and no other option; the practice eval counts those apart and asks Jev the same of every sort, `jevGiveaway` (from 0.8 nearly all real; on meanings, gaps and pairs it was noise), to show where the code check misses one or flags a good one; with a TypeSafe key the extension asks it too, and a sort it flags is written once more, the second kept either way (`background.ts`, like the key check), and the drill tests run it on every drill), pictures (`picture`, `yes_no`: the model writes a deck of `DECK_SIZE` emoji with their words, `pictureDeck`, never the answer, and code builds the exercise from its first picture; the tab builds the next picture exercises from the rest with no request, `ExerciseType.deck`, in page mode only on the same section), `key` (with a TypeSafe key, Jev checks a spot-the-form, gap-fill or particles exercise's key in one request, every word, option or sentence-and-particle a question, `KEY_THRESHOLD`; one it disagrees with is written once more and the second kept either way; `npm run eval -- exercise`, its `batched` checker), `session` (the answer and grading), `hint` ("💡 Hint", not for pairs, conversations, speaking or drills, `hasHint`: Haiku writes one as each exercise arrives, so it shows at once; dropped if it quotes the answer, and with a TypeSafe key if Jev thinks it gives the answer away or leads elsewhere, `HINT_THRESHOLD`, not yet evaluated; one more try, then "No hint for this one."), `view` (`fillPractice`), `styles`, `controller` (`practiceTab`). `npm run eval -- practice` (lesson mode only for now, exercises planned by `schedule.ts`, each headed with its point; the `open` variant leaves the point to the model, as before) writes `evals/out/practice.md` and the raw responses to `practice.json`; variants in `evals/variants/practice.ts`
- `src/practice/drills/`: drills on a language's sounds or script in its first four lessons (lesson mode; culture lessons have none and aren't counted, `drillLessons`), and on what a later lesson teaches in that lesson (`Drill.taught`: in Japanese, the time, counting people, dates, verb groups and the て-form, and counters, each in the lesson that teaches it), built by code from per-language data (`<code>.ts`: letter tables with look-alikes, loanwords, minimal pairs, sorts by sound, Hangul blocks, Arabic words to join, words read without vowels, pinyin tone pairs, silent letters, accents (acute, or `mark`), lenition after what lenites, a spelling rule, questions answered with their verb, the article by gender, counters with their readings, small kana, kana said otherwise (は as わ), words sorted by group, a form to make, numbers read their own way, this and that by distance, set phrases by situation, the time on a clock, answers to a negative question (doch), adjective endings in phrases built by code from a declension table; `types.ts`), with no model call and no hint. `build.ts` has the 34 drills (letter to sound, which letter, a loanword to read, Russian with untaught letters romanised, stress, kana pairs, building a block, vowel signs, joining letters, sun and moon letters, without vowels, tones, which word, sort by sound, silent letters, accents, dictation, lenition, lenite or not, spelling rule, answer with the verb, the article, telling the time, yes, no or doch, adjective endings, which counter, count it, small kana, said otherwise, sort them, make the form, read it, near or far, what do you say), each with when it fits a lesson; `drillsFor(lesson)` gives a lesson's (`PracticeDeps.drills`), and `planNext` makes about `DRILL_SHARE` (`plan.ts`) of exercises one. Script drills show the letters bare (`Prompt.plain`: no readings, romaji or 🔊 until answered); new kinds `units` (tap letters or syllables) and `write` (typed, strict about accents, `sameSpelling`); an `order` can show what its tiles make (`preview`: Arabic joined, a Hangul block, `src/hangul.ts`). Listening drills need a voice, so Gaelic gets only read ones, in its first five lessons (`DrillData.lessons`). `tests/practice/drills.test.ts` builds every drill in every lesson it fits
- Speaking exercises (opt-in: Options → "Speaking exercises", which asks for the microphone there, plus a DeepInfra key): Practice's `say` type (`speaking: true`, about one in five exercises, `SPEAKING_SHARE` in `src/practice/plan.ts`) shows a sentence to say aloud. `entrypoints/offscreen/` records it (the microphone is the extension's, not the page's), the background sends it to Whisper large-v3 on DeepInfra with the sentence as its prompt (`src/listen.ts`; silence isn't sent, since Whisper prompted can "hear" the sentence in it), and `src/practice/speaking.ts` marks it leniently: words matched fuzzily and in order, great (80%) or almost (50%) pass, else try again, the words not heard greyed out
- Progress (`src/progress/progress.ts`, pure; `entrypoints/progress/`, a page opened from the popup's "Progress" link): lesson mode's practice, from the ledger (`src/practice/ledger.ts`) and each lesson's cached kit for its topics' labels and words' glosses (any older kit of the lesson if the current one isn't built). A topic or word is **learned** when answered right last time and right on two days or more, **needs work** when answered wrong last time or wrong more often than right, else **practising**. The page: a recap (this week against the week before, what was right for the first time this week, totals), a calendar of 26 weeks (a day counts towards the streak with `DAY_GOAL` exercises), levels (lessons done and topics learned per level; the one worked on is the first with a lesson not done), milestones (dated where the ledger can tell), what needs work across lessons, and each lesson's topics and words. It draws again when the ledger changes in another tab. Words are the exercises' focus words, so a word's right or wrong is the exercise's, a weaker signal than a topic's
