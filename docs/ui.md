# UI on the page

What Language Weave draws on the page; all of it in a shadow root outside `<body>`, so it's never woven.

- `src/widget.ts`: the floating panel for the passage (fixed position, shadow root outside `<body>` so it's never woven); `fillPiece` draws a piece and its quizzes (true or false, gap fill, match the words, guess what), keeping answers in a `QuizState` across redraws
- Clicking a woven word flips it to English and back. 🔊 beside it (`src/wordButton.ts`) reads it aloud, slowly (`WORD_SPEAKING_RATE` in `src/speech.ts`, also used by the widget's word lists): fetched on the click (about $0.0002 a word) and kept for the page by the speaker. A word with no voice (or no recording) has no 🔊 (`content.ts`, `canSay`)
- `src/wordButton.ts`: buttons beside the woven word under the pointer: 🔊, ☆ saves it to learn, ✓ marks it known (toggles, `src/vocab/`, each explained on hover by `STATUS_TIPS`); a click on the word flips it to English (a peek). Same shadow-root rules as the widget
- `src/vocab/`: the reader's words, one store per language in `chrome.storage.local` (`vocab|<lang>`, keyed by `normalizeLemma`), across pages and kept across a restore: `store` (pure: each word's status, **seen**, **saved** or **known**, pages it was woven on, peeks at its English, listens, lesson mode's practice answers, and for saved and known words up to `MAX_CONTEXTS` sentences it was met in; a practice word ("le chien") is found by its citation form; known by itself (`PROMOTION`) after 5 listens, or right in practice (right last time); known stays known, peeks and wrong answers or not, until the reader unmarks it, which counts afresh; Anki CSV), `context` (`sentenceAround`: a woven word's sentence as the page shows it), `tracker` (the content script's side, in either mode: each woven word counted once a page, a flip to English a peek, a listen from 🔊 beside a woven word or the widget's word lists (`onWordSaid`; in lesson mode a word of the lesson's textbook or kit is added, and listed on the Words page after `LISTED_LISTENS` listens), practice answers with the kit's glosses, ☆ and ✓ from the word button and the popup's list (`vocab-set`); the popup counts its own listens; sightings written together after `FLUSH_MS`). Known words are still woven, and no model call uses the store yet. `entrypoints/words/` (opened from the popup's "Words" link): saved, known and seen words, each with 🔊, a definition and an example sentence (with its own 🔊 and its English), search, ☆ and ✓, Anki export. Definitions and examples (`src/vocab/definitions.ts`) are written by Haiku in batches of `DEFINE_BATCH` the first time a word is shown there (seen words once their fold is opened; the gloss stands in until then), and kept in storage (`definitions`, by language, lemma and `DEFINE_VERSION`). Every list is latest first (`byRecent`: the day first met, then the order added). The widget's last tab, Words (after Passage and Practice, in both modes; `src/vocab/wordsTab.ts`), shows the `WORDS_TAB_SHOWN` latest the same way (`src/vocab/wordRows.ts`), from the page's `WordTracker`, drawn again each time it's opened.
- `src/notes.ts`: part of the hidden reading widget below, so not shown: summaries of a highlight, opened from a "Summarise" button at its end: a note in the page margin beside the paragraph, or a bubble under the highlight when there's no margin (`placeNote`); same shadow-root rules as the widget
- `src/reading/`: the old reading-format widget, hidden (`SHOW_READING_WIDGET` in `content.ts`) but kept: `formats.ts` (4 generic formats filled from a lesson's `brief`, plus the summary of a highlighted passage; prompts, parser), `collect.ts` (what's on screen, headings, woven words, the highlight), `controller.ts` (picker, cache, refresh on scroll). `npm run eval -- reading` writes every format × lesson × page to `evals/out/reading.md`
