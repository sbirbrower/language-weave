# Modes

Page mode and lesson mode, and where each kind of lesson lives.

- Two modes, switched in the popup (`Settings.mode`): **page** (default) weaves the page's words (Options → "Words to weave", themed by default), or with "Weave the page" unchecked (`Settings.weavePage`) only picks them for the widget and leaves the page in English and the widget writes about the section on screen (or the text the reader highlights) with those words and one grammar point for the reader's level (`Settings.level`, set in the popup), with a Practice tab of exercises on that section; **lesson** teaches a syllabus lesson with scenes on its theme, never the page, has a Practice tab of exercises on the lesson, one at a time (`src/practice/`), and marks the page where the lesson applies (`src/marks/`) or weaves it only when the popup's "Mark this page" or "Weave this page" button asks, for that page only. Its widget follows the reader: while it's open, switching tab (or loading a page in the tab) opens it there by itself (`followTo` in `background.ts`; it needs every site, `<all_urls>`, which is optional in the manifest and asked for when the reader picks lesson mode or clicks "Follow me to other tabs" in the popup, `src/follow.ts`; without it the widget follows only to tabs the reader clicked Language Weave on), as they left it: the tab, the piece and its answers, the exercise, its answer and the next one, with nothing new written and no marks or weave (`src/lessonState.ts`, kept in `chrome.storage.session`; the tab the reader is in saves after each change and as they leave; closing it closes it everywhere)
- `src/syllabus/`: syllabus lessons (title, level, points), one file per language (`fr.ts`, `de.ts`, `ja.ts`, `zh.ts`, `ko.ts`, `hi.ts`, `he.ts`, `ar.ts`, `cs.ts`, `es.ts`, `pt.ts`, `ru.ts`, `it.ts`, `gd.ts`). Chosen in the popup in lesson mode; taught only in the widget, never by the weave. Each language also has five culture and geography lessons (`kind: 'culture'`, titled "Culture: …" and "Geography: …"), like a textbook's culture pages: each follows the lesson whose words and grammar it uses and teaches the places and customs where the language is spoken, with their words, and no new grammar. The kit, textbook and practice prompts treat them like any other lesson, since their points carry the facts; they have no code-built drills, and drills count lessons without them (`drillLessons`)
- Lessons the reader writes (`src/syllabus/custom.ts`): the popup's lesson list ends with "New lesson…", which opens `entrypoints/lessons/` (also where they're changed or deleted). The reader gives a language, a level, a topic and as many details as they like; one Opus call (thinking on, structured output, ~4¢, 15s median in the eval) writes it as a syllabus lesson like the course's: title, points, and `notes` (the request's specifics the points don't hold: themes, words, emphases, what to leave out), plus a `left_out` line shown only to the reader. They check and edit it, then save it to `chrome.storage.local` (`customLessons`, `src/syllabus/stored.ts`, with the request kept to write it again). It's listed under "Your lessons" after the course's, and the kit, textbook, passages, practice and marks are built from it like any other; `notes` reach the kit and textbook prompts only when set, so the course's own lessons keep their prompts, stamps and caches. Its id has `-custom-`, it has no code-built drills, and the background looks lessons up with `findLesson` (the course's, then the reader's) and sends the content script the lesson in `weave-config`. `npm run eval -- custom-lesson` writes `evals/out/custom-lesson.md`; variants in `evals/variants/custom-lesson.ts`
- `src/grammar/`: page-mode grammar points by level, one file per language (`fr.ts`, `de.ts`, `ja.ts`, `zh.ts`, `ko.ts`, `hi.ts`, `he.ts`, `ar.ts`, `cs.ts`, `es.ts`, `pt.ts`, `ru.ts`, `it.ts`, `gd.ts`); one is picked per passage
